The same owned receiving process can signal its retained imported event through a second XPC connection after the sender invalidates the first connection and clears its original event and handle variables. Dropping the peer's explicit event property instead leaves the sender's gate at 3 and work pending; its retained recovery alias then explicitly releases value7 and completes exact bytes. This establishes a finite retained-object behavior across local connection invalidation, not sole ownership or physical release.
