A new owned watcher extends the birth/path-pinned kernel exit observer with a one-second stopped-state gate. For stopped controls it requests the owned child to stop itself, repeatedly validates identity until public libproc reports SSTOP4, registers exit events, then rechecks that stopped state before releasing root exit. Running contrasts are independently confirmed nonstopped. The helper sends no signals; actual drain remains the existing supervisor's SIGTERM/SIGKILL policy.
