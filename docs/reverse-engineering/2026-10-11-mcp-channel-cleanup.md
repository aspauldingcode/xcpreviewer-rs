## Limits and next work

Session.close is still trusted to settle. An arbitrary non-settling adapter prevents channel.done from completing; no new timeout or physical resource termination guarantee is claimed. A successful adapter return is also not independent physical reclamation evidence. This fix makes rejection observable; it does not retry a failed adapter, kill a process, or invent resource release. Native R1/R2, correlated capture and both milestones remain open. Next choose a genuinely independent demanded acceptance boundary, or an owned process watchdog for non-settling adapters if packaging requires it; skip completed finite cleanup permutations.
