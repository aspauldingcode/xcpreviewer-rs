# Native alpha oracle and quantization counterexample

An owned SwiftUI ImageRenderer fixture now captures explicit sRGB red at zero, quarter, half and full opacity, plus red over opaque blue. Geometry is 17×9, scale1, Aqua, renderer `.nonLinear`. Each capture is a fresh subprocess. PNG decoding compares every straight RGBA byte without normalization. This is a separate native oracle, not Xcode Canvas capture or transport evidence.

The initial prediction independently rounded both half-composite channels to128. Native output is `(128,0,127,255)`, contradicting that prediction in every pixel of both half-composite captures. Ten of twelve other/full comparisons passed and all six same-input pairs were identical. The sealed initial result remains inconclusive under the controller's conservative classification; its byte evidence specifically falsifies symmetric half rounding. An earlier16MiB artifact cap interrupted module-cache compilation; that failed seal is retained and quarantined: compiler-cache artifacts changed after sealing, so its integrity check fails. The three subsequent completed seals verify unchanged. A dedicated owned subprocess/cache quiescence experiment is now the highest-priority graph task. The bounded artifact allowance was raised to256MiB, without raising compile/capture deadlines.

The revised competing model quantizes source alpha to an8-bit integer before opaque source-over: red=`floor(255*a+0.5)`, blue=`255-red`. Before execution, eighth and three-quarter opacity were added as holdouts. Predictions `(32,0,223,255)` and `(191,0,64,255)` passed along with all prior cases, twice per case in each of two independently sealed campaigns. Total19,584 decoded RGBA bytes match predictions. This supports the bounded outputs and discriminates the two formulas here; it does not uniquely identify the internal implementation, arbitrary-color rounding, linear rendering, premultiplication at every boundary or color-profile behavior. Exported PNGs contain no ICC profile in these observations.

Verifier mutation controls flip first/last pixel channels and add/remove a tail byte. Complete decoded pixels are checked; PNG compressed encodings, underlying native stride/padding and unused graphics allocation bytes are outside this oracle's scope. Owned PNG goldens and their hashes are published; generated executables/module caches remain local. No Xcode window was opened.
